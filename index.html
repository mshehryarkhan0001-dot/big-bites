Our Story

Fast Food, Cooked Properly

Big Bites started as a single griddle and a stubborn idea: fast food doesn't have to mean lazy food. Every patty is hand-smashed to order, every wing is double-fried for crunch, and nothing sits under a heat lamp waiting for you.

We built this place for the 1am cravings and the after-work rush alike — loud flavours, honest prices, and a kitchen that never slows down.
